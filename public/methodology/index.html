METHODOLOGY & TRUST

Make the evidence trail visible.

CF Investing is designed as an analytical and educational platform. Trust depends on showing where information comes from, how it is reviewed, what is modeled and where the platform stops.

Core principles

Source → Review → Interpretation → Decision support.

Data provenance

Material facts should identify their source, date and publication status whenever practical.

Assumption transparency

Modeled outputs should distinguish user inputs, simplifying assumptions and calculated results.

Review status

Public-source information, sponsor-provided information and independently reviewed evidence should never be presented as equivalent.

Project evidence framework

Use specific review states instead of a generic “verified” label.

Public Source→Source Reviewed→Financial Review→Technical / Legal / ESG Review

Public Source

Facts are derived from an official public entity, development institution, issuer, sponsor or other identified source.

Source Reviewed

CF Investing has reviewed source identity, date and the consistency of the facts reproduced on the platform.

Specialist Review

Where used in the future, financial, technical, legal or ESG reviews should identify the responsible specialist and the scope of work.

Editorial & data policy

What readers should be able to understand.

Editorial methodology

Research products should distinguish factual evidence, interpretation, scenario analysis and opinion.

Freshness

Project and market data can change. Material pages should display dates or review timing when information is time-sensitive.

Corrections

Material factual errors should be corrected when identified, with important changes documented when appropriate.

Conflicts & sponsorship

Sponsored content, commercial relationships or partner contributions should be labeled clearly and separated from independent editorial analysis.

Financial-services boundary

Analysis is not transaction execution.

CF Investing may explain financial instruments, compare public information, provide educational tools and refer users to external sources or authorized third parties. The platform does not currently receive investment orders, custody client funds, execute securities transactions or represent the project cases shown on the site as investments offered through CF Investing.

Future commercial features: directories, sponsorships, partner referrals or transaction-adjacent services should be introduced only after a specific legal and regulatory review of the proposed activity, remuneration model, jurisdiction and partner responsibilities.

Tools methodology

First-pass models should never hide their simplifications.

Inputs

Users should be able to see and change the assumptions that drive the result.

Outputs

Results should be presented as indicative analytical outputs rather than definitive recommendations.

Limitations

Each tool should state the major elements omitted from its simplified model and indicate when deeper analysis is required.

CF Investing Trust
