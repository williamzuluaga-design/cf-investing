PRIVACY & NEWSLETTER

A simple notice for the data CF Investing currently uses.

CF Investing is designed to minimize personal-data collection. The main current collection point is the optional CF Investing Brief newsletter signup.

Newsletter signup

When you submit the newsletter form, CF Investing uses your email address to request newsletter enrollment, send the confirmation flow and deliver future editions if you confirm. The form also sends basic attribution such as page source, language and broad content interest so newsletter performance can be understood without building a detailed personal profile.

By submitting the form and confirming the email, you authorize this processing for newsletter purposes. You can withdraw from future emails at any time using the unsubscribe mechanism included in Beehiiv messages.

Service providers

CF Investing uses Beehiiv to manage newsletter subscriptions and delivery. The CF Investing website and subscription endpoint run on Cloudflare infrastructure. These providers process information as necessary to provide their respective services and are subject to their own contractual and privacy terms.

CF Investing does not publish newsletter subscriber email addresses and does not use the newsletter signup to execute investments, financing or brokerage activity.

Browser-local features

The Executive Watchlist stores selected topics and comparison snapshots in your browser using local storage. Those local Watchlist preferences are not sent to CF Investing by the Watchlist feature itself.

Retention and deletion

Newsletter information may remain in Beehiiv while the subscription is active and as required to maintain suppression or unsubscribe records. To stop future newsletter emails, use the unsubscribe link in a CF Investing Brief message. For other privacy requests, reply to a CF Investing Brief message so the request can be associated with the relevant subscription record.

Changes

This notice may be updated as CF Investing adds analytics, accounts, forms or other data-processing features. Material new collection should be documented before it is activated.
