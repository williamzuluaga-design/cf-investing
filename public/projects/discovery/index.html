Discovery architecture

Official source → structured extraction → deduplication → human review.

Official source→Source-specific connector→Candidate metadata→Review queue→Human decision
Boundary: connectors collect selected metadata and links. They do not infer undisclosed facts, republish full source pages or write directly to the public project dataset.

Connector coverage

BID Invest · XML

Official XML database with project number, country, publication date and other fields when available. HTML catalogue remains as fallback.

CAF · field extractor

Source-specific catalogue extraction for operation number, country, approval/update dates, risk type, financing instrument, sector and status when visible.

Bancóldex · field extractor

Source-specific extraction for financing-program date, available facility, company size, use of proceeds, sector and geographic coverage.

IFC & Findeter

Registered for later source-specific integrations. They remain inactive rather than being forced through a low-quality generic parser.
