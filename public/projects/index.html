Project profile standard

Move from deal lists to decision-grade structure.

Identity & stage

Project, sponsor, jurisdiction, sector, permits, counterparties and current development stage.

Economics & capital

CAPEX, capital need, committed financing, revenue model, CFADS, debt capacity and return assumptions when disclosed.

Risk & contracts

Construction, demand, FX, rate, regulatory, technology, EPC, O&M and offtake considerations.

Impact & evidence

Climate/social metrics, source date, freshness, review status and the next milestone required for readiness.

Platform role

Discovery + data + comparison + screening + education.

CF Investing currently adds structure and analysis around public information. Financing or investment transactions, if introduced in the future, should be conducted with duly authorized third parties under a separately reviewed commercial and regulatory model.
