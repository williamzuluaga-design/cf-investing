Project profile standard

What a future verified project card should disclose.

Project & Sponsor

Economics

CAPEX, revenue model, financing need, cash-flow profile, IRR/NPV assumptions and sensitivity ranges.

Key risks, mitigants, ESG or climate contribution, expected outputs, SDGs and verification status.

Future real project profiles should include source dates, verification status and a clear distinction between sponsor-provided information and independently validated information.
