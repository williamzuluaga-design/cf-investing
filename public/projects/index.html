Project profile standard

Move from deal lists to evidence records.

Identity & stage

Project, sponsor, jurisdiction, sector, source date and current development or financing stage.

Economics & capital

Disclosed financing, CAPEX, capital need, revenue model and financial metrics without filling gaps with invented assumptions.

Risk & contracts

Construction, demand, FX, rates, regulation, technology and material contracts when evidence is available.

Impact & provenance

Environmental/social information, source URL, freshness, evidence status and explicit gaps for deeper diligence.
