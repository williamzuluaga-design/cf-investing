PROJECT INTELLIGENCE · METHODOLOGY

Turn scattered project disclosures into comparable evidence.

CF Investing uses a structured workflow to discover projects, extract facts, normalize fields, record provenance and separate public-source evidence from deeper review.

Pipeline

Discovery → extraction → normalization → review → monitoring.

1. Discovery

Prioritize primary sources such as development banks, public agencies, regulated platforms, issuers and project sponsors. Secondary media may help discover cases but should not be the preferred source for material facts.

2. Extraction

Capture project identity, sponsor or borrower, geography, sector, development stage, financing instrument, amount, source URL, source date and material terms actually disclosed.

3. Normalization

Map source terminology into a common CF Investing taxonomy so cases can be filtered and compared without changing the underlying factual meaning.

4. Review

Check source identity, date, project naming and the consistency of the facts reproduced. Do not infer undisclosed CAPEX, returns, contracts or risk mitigants.

5. Monitoring

Record the last review date and revisit time-sensitive cases. Status, amounts and approval dates may change after the original source publication.

6. Specialist diligence

Financial, technical, legal or ESG review is a separate layer and should only be shown when the scope and responsible reviewer are identified.

Evidence states

Describe the review performed, not a blanket certification.

Public Source→Source Reviewed→Financial Review→Technical / Legal / ESG Review

Public Source

The project or financing fact appears in an identified public, institutional, issuer or sponsor source.

Source Reviewed

CF Investing has checked the source identity, publication timing and consistency of the facts reproduced in the profile.

Specialist Review

Reserved for clearly scoped analysis by a qualified financial, technical, legal or ESG specialist. One review does not imply review of every dimension.

Project profile schema

A common evidence model.

Identity

Name, sponsor/borrower, jurisdiction, location, sector and project type.

Stage

Development or financing stage, key milestone and source date.

Capital

Instrument, disclosed amount, capital need and committed capital when available.

Economics

Revenue model, CAPEX, cash flow, debt capacity and return metrics only when disclosed or explicitly modeled.

Risk & contracts

Construction, demand, offtake, FX, rates, regulatory, technology, EPC and O&M evidence when available.

Impact & provenance

Environmental/social information, source URL, source date, last review date and evidence status.

Automation rules

Scrape carefully; publish only reviewed facts.

A future ingestion process may automate discovery and extraction, but publication should preserve source attribution, terms-of-use constraints, rate limits, copyright boundaries and a human-review checkpoint. The value of CF Investing is normalization and analysis—not wholesale republication of third-party content.
