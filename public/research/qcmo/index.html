Editorial framework

QCMO as a bulletin-school.

The initiative is designed to support first publications by students, monitors, research-seed participants and young researchers from Universidad de Medellín and Universidad EAN, strengthening analytical and communication skills through guided financial dissemination.

Macroeconomics

Inflation, rates, GDP and employment as the operating context for households, firms and markets.

Markets

Fixed income, equities, FX, commodities and international financial conditions.

Financial education

Accessible explanations that connect market developments with practical financial decisions.
