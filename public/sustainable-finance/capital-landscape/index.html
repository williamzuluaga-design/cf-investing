Sankey-style capital system map

Sources → intermediaries → instruments → institutions and projects.

This visualization emphasizes how different forms of capital can move through a financing system. Blended and catalytic finance appear as mechanisms within the system rather than a separate asset class.

How to use the map

Start with the capital need

Growth, project construction, capital strengthening, risk reduction and project preparation require different instruments.

Separate source from instrument

A development institution can provide direct debt, intermediate through a bank, support a guarantee or anchor a capital-market structure.

Look for the constraint

Where commercial finance is not scaling, concessional or catalytic resources may be more useful for preparation, de-risking or affordability than as permanent substitutes for private capital.

Source boundary

Measured KPI data and analytical topology are kept separate.

Global KPI values come from cited sources. The Sankey link widths are qualitative visual-emphasis weights created by CF Investing to explain the financing system and should not be interpreted as measured capital volumes.
