Decision logic

Sector sets the baseline; stage and risk reshape the capital stack.

Sector

Defines common financing channels and the types of capital providers most likely to have a relevant mandate.

Stage

Changes the evidence burden: early projects need preparation and feasibility support, while construction and operating assets require stronger financial and contractual evidence.

Barrier

Highlights why commercial capital may not yet scale—construction, revenue, FX/rates, policy, adaptation economics or institutional capacity.

Boundary

Navigation is not recommendation.

The output is intentionally generic. It does not select a named lender, estimate approval probability or replace project-specific financial, technical, legal, environmental or social due diligence.
