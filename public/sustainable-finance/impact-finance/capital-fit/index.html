Scoring method

A transparent heuristic based only on published fields.

Institution fit · 30%

Does the funder's published target list include the selected type of institution?

Need / instrument fit · 30%

Does the funder publish at least one instrument associated with the selected financing need?

Impact focus · 20%

Does the selected priority appear in the funder's published sector or impact focus?

Ticket · 15%

Does the indicative amount fall inside the published minimum/maximum when available?

Currency · 5%

Does the selected currency appear in the funder's published currency options?

Only selected criteria enter the denominator. A high score means higher compatibility with the mapped fields, not higher probability of receiving financing.

Source boundary

Verify current conditions directly with each funder.

The underlying mapping is a useful screening source, but the guide explicitly notes that funders can change instruments, eligibility rules and geographic coverage. CF Investing therefore links to official funder websites and does not present the output as eligibility confirmation.
