Instruments

Different risks require different capital structures.

DebtGreen loans, project finance and sustainability-linked lending.
Capital marketsGreen, social, sustainability and transition bonds.
EquityInfrastructure, private equity, venture and impact capital.
Risk mitigationGuarantees, first-loss capital, concessional tranches and insurance.
Catalytic logic: concessional or risk-bearing capital matters when it changes the risk-return profile enough to mobilize additional commercial capital—not simply because a project has a sustainable label.

Project lens

What makes a sustainable opportunity financeable?

Impact case

Clear mitigation, adaptation, resilience, environmental or social outcome with credible measurement.

Financial case

Visible cash flows, realistic CAPEX/OPEX, return profile, debt capacity and downside analysis.

Risk allocation

Construction, demand, regulatory, currency, technology and counterparty risks assigned to parties able to manage them.
