GLOBAL CONTEXT · 2026

US$2T+

Annual global climate finance has crossed the US$2 trillion mark. The central question is no longer only how much capital exists, but how effectively it reaches bankable mitigation, adaptation and resilience projects.

Reference: Climate Policy Initiative, Global Landscape of Climate Finance 2026.

FINANCIAL INSTRUMENTS

Different risks require different capital structures.

DebtGreen loans, project finance, sustainability-linked lending.
Capital marketsGreen, social, sustainability and transition bonds.
EquityInfrastructure, private equity, venture and impact capital.
De-riskingGuarantees, first-loss capital, concessional tranches and insurance.
Bankability matters. Sustainable projects often fail to attract capital not because the theme is unattractive, but because project preparation, risk allocation, revenue visibility or capital structure are insufficient. This is where catalytic and blended finance can be decisive.

PROJECT LENS

What makes a sustainable opportunity financeable?

01

Impact case

Clear mitigation, adaptation, resilience, environmental or social outcome with a credible measurement logic.

02

Financial case

Visible cash flows, realistic CAPEX/OPEX, return profile, debt capacity and downside scenario.

03

Risk allocation

Construction, demand, regulatory, currency, technology and counterparty risks assigned to parties able to manage them.
