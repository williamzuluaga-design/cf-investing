Impact finance · Colombia

Funding access is a readiness problem as much as a capital-availability problem.

Sector guidance for Colombian microfinance institutions highlights four defining elements of impact finance: intentionality, additionality, measurement and financial return. It also shows that funders evaluate financial strength together with portfolio quality, governance, risk management, legal compliance, measurable impact and ESG practices.

Instruments

Different needs require different financing structures.

CreditLoans and credit lines for portfolio growth, liquidity and institutional expansion.
Capital strengtheningSubordinated debt, equity and quasi-equity where institutions need stronger capitalization or flexibility.
Risk mitigationGuarantees and blended structures that can reduce risk or crowd in additional capital.
CapabilitiesTechnical assistance for governance, digitization, processes, impact measurement and institutional strengthening.
Instrument-fit principle: there is no single “best” product. Purpose, institutional maturity, capital structure, debt capacity, currency, tenor and funder conditions should be assessed together.
