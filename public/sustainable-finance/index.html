Global climate-finance context

More capital is flowing, but allocation and risk remain central.

Climate Policy Initiative's Global Landscape of Climate Finance 2026 reports that global climate finance surpassed US$2 trillion for the first time in 2024, with approximately US$2.1 trillion estimated for 2025. Mitigation finance reached about US$1.9 trillion in 2024 while tracked adaptation finance remained around US$64 billion.

Colombia ecosystem

Regulation, coordination, knowledge and capital operate together.

The Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia identifies green finance and environmental/climate-risk management as strategic priorities. The Mesa de Finanzas Sostenibles de Colombia brings together financial-system actors, authorities and international partners, while the Centro Regional de Finanzas Sostenibles at Universidad de los Andes contributes research, training and convening.

CF Investing uses these institutions as ecosystem reference points. Inclusion on the Actors Map does not imply partnership or endorsement.
