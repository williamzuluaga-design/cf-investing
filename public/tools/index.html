Sustainable finance

Research first, tool second.

Catalytic and blended finance remain part of the Sustainable & Climate Finance research program and capital-landscape framework. A dedicated calculator should only be introduced once the methodology and supporting evidence are strong enough to justify it.

Design principle

Simple enough to explore. Transparent enough to question.

Inputs

Users can see and change the assumptions driving each result.

Outputs

Results are indicative analytical outputs, not automatic recommendations.

Limitations

Each tool should explain material simplifications and when deeper analysis is required.
