One rule across all tools

Use the output to ask better questions.

A valuation, bankability score or investor profile is a starting point, not a decision. The tools are designed to expose assumptions and sensitivity before more detailed modeling or professional review.

What these tools do not do

They do not provide personalized investment advice, approve credit, certify a valuation, execute trades, custody assets or replace financial, legal, tax, technical or ESG due diligence.
