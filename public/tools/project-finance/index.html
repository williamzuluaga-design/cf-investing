How to interpret

Four signals, not one verdict.

Leverage

Higher debt can improve equity efficiency but also raises refinancing and repayment risk. The appropriate level depends on sector, contract quality and cash-flow stability.

DSCR

Debt-service coverage compares operating cash available for debt service with scheduled debt payments. A value above 1 means modeled cash flow covers modeled debt service.

Project IRR

The estimated project IRR is based on unlevered project cash flows in this simplified tool. It should be compared with risk-adjusted hurdle rates and scenario results.
