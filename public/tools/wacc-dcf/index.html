How to interpret

Value depends on assumptions, not just formulas.

WACC

The weighted average cost of capital reflects the opportunity cost demanded by debt and equity providers. It should be consistent with project or company risk, currency and cash-flow definition.

Terminal Value

A high terminal-value share means the valuation depends heavily on long-run assumptions. That is a signal to stress-test growth and discount-rate assumptions more aggressively.

Equity Value

Enterprise value represents operating assets. Equity value is derived after subtracting net debt and should be reconciled with non-operating assets and other claims in a full valuation.
