TraderCapital heritage

What is reused — and what is intentionally excluded.

Reused

Risk profiling, editable allocation, portfolio analytics, strategy comparison, transparency of metrics and an accessible digital-investing experience.

Educational simulation

Users can test hypothetical portfolios and understand return, volatility, Sharpe ratio, drawdown and diversification before making real-world decisions.

Not included

No brokerage, wallet, custody, KYC onboarding, copy-trading execution, crowdfunding, crypto trading or automated personalized recommendations.

Learning loop

Profile → allocate → compare → reflect.

The WealthTech Lab is designed as a repeatable learning loop rather than a one-click recommendation engine. A future version can add rebalancing exercises, historical scenarios and classroom assignments while keeping execution outside CF Investing.

Research bridge

From WealthTech product design to evidence.

The lab connects with CF Investing Research to support future analysis of robo-advisory, social investing, digital brokerage, portfolio automation and investor behavior.
