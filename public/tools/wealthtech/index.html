TraderCapital heritage

What is reused — and what is intentionally excluded.

Reused

Risk profiling, portfolio analytics, strategy comparison, transparency of metrics and an accessible digital-investing experience.

Educational simulation

Users can compare hypothetical portfolios and understand return, volatility, Sharpe ratio and drawdown before making real-world decisions.

Not included

No brokerage, wallet, custody, KYC onboarding, copy-trading execution, crowdfunding, crypto trading or automated personalized recommendations.
